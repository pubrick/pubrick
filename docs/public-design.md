# Public website art direction

## Audience and position

Pubrick serves people who work with ideas: independent writers, small studios,
brand editors and agency teams. Its distinctive promise is content automation around the creator’s selected
sources, brand voice, formats, prompts and cadence. Human review is a delivery
step; a draft is a workflow status, not the purpose of the product. It is also inspectable, self-hostable
open-source software. Neither identity is a decorative badge.

## Research applied

In its [evaluation of AI prototypes](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-prototyping/),
Nielsen Norman Group identifies generic visual output and contextually misplaced
patterns, along with weak hierarchy, grouping and spacing. We assess the actual
rendered page and its decisions rather than accepting surface polish.

[Designpixil's creative-director critique](https://designpixil.com/blog/ai-slop-design)
describes interchangeable card rows, stock visual effects, generic writing and
lack of product evidence. This is a practitioner's critique, not a formal
definition or a validated detector. No single color, font or layout proves
that a page is AI slop.

## Decisions for Pubrick

- Keep the existing brick-orange logo and semantic contrast-tested colors.
  The public site belongs to the same product as the workspace.
- Use an editorial opening and readable typography. Serif text has a specific
  role in the studio-note example; body and navigation retain the product font.
- Show a concrete piece of work: a short studio note and creative rules and a content preview
  that visitors can edit locally. Use the real editor component rather than
  drawing an invented dashboard or suggesting a live model call occurred.
- Group the explanation around sources, creative rules and publishing rhythm. Avoid equal-size
  decorative feature cards and repeated sales headings when a clear editorial
  list communicates the relationships better.
- State facts: supported delivery paths, human review, BYOK, AGPL and actual
  hosted availability. Do not manufacture customers, ratings, prices or results.
- Keep navigation predictable, touch targets usable, focus visible and mobile
  reading comfortable. Personality must survive responsive layouts and dark mode.

No gratuitous motion, glowing background objects, fake handwriting, paper-noise
textures or branded imitation are needed. Each visual choice must explain the
work or establish Pubrick's own identity. Inspect real screenshots at mobile
and desktop widths before judging the result.
